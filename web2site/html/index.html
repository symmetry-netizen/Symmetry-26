01 ABOUT SYMMETRY

Where rigorous thought meets imagination.

Symmetry '26 is the annual mathematics festival organised by the Department of Mathematics, Presidency University, Kolkata.

The festival brings mathematical ideas out of the classroom through competitions, creative challenges, research presentations, talks and conversations.

02 FEATURED SPEAKERS

Ideas worth listening to.

Conversations at the intersection of mathematics, computation, and the sciences.

01

Retired Professor · ISI, Kolkata     |     Visiting Professor · IACS, Kolkata

Prof. Alok Goswami

"A Masterstroke from a Mathematical Genius"

He is a distinguished Mathematician and statistician, known for his research in Probability, Stochastic processes, Mathematical statistics, and Statistical Inference. He is a former Professor at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, and has made significant contributions to both Research and the teaching of advanced Mathematics and Probabillity.

02

Head of AI Engineering · Kenvue

Mr. Biswajit Pal

"The Math Behind ChatGPT: From Linear Algebra to Attention"

He is a Seasoned AI and Data Science Leader with over 20 years of industry experience. He currently leads AI and Data Science initiatives at Kenvue, working across Machine learning, generative AI, Data Engineering and Digital transformation. He is also a recognized industry thought leader, with Multiple patents, publications and awards for his contribution to AI innovation.

03

Director · Consilious Software Consulting LLP

Mr. Arindam Das

"Indoor Localization Under Uncertainity: An Evidence-Based Approach to Target Tracking"

He is the Director at Conslious Software Consulting LLP, with more than 25 years of experience working across software, electronics, embedded systems, IOT, and Industrial Technology. A recurring theme throughout his work has been the interaction between physical world and computation-how systems can measure physical phenomena, convert those measurements into data, and use software and mathematical models to extract useful information.
