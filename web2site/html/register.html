Guide HOW TO REGISTER

Steps to a confirmed seat.

Registration is a two-part process — payment first, then the registration form. Read through the steps below before you begin.

  1. 01

    Complete your payment

    Head over to the payment section and complete the registration fee payment. This has to be done before you can proceed with registration — the form will ask for your payment details.

  2. 02

    Save your payment proof

    During the payment gateway process, keep your payment confirmation document and the SBI payment reference number handy — both are required later in the registration process, so save them or take a screenshot as soon as the payment goes through.

  3. 03

    Fill out the registration form

    Follow and complete the registration form with all necessary details, along with the required file uploads — payment proof and your college ID — to finish your registration.

  4. 04

    Enrol into events

    Once your registration is confirmed, use the Events section on the homepage to enrol into the individual programmes you wish to participate in.

  5. 05

    Need help?

    If you run into any issues along the way, reach out through the contact section on the homepage — we're happy to help.
